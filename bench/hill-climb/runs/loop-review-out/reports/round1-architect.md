### Architect findings — round 1
A-1 | blocker | ratelimit/limiter.py:30-33 vs :38-47 | State ownership is split: `_snapshot` (:30-33) owns the lock but returns a copy, and `allow` (:43,:46) writes `_buckets` and `stats` (:44,:47) without it. The lock therefore protects nothing that matters; the invariant "tokens for a key are decremented at most burst times per refill" lives in two places with no single critical section. | Over-admission under load; the thread-safety claim in the module docstring (:4) is false. | Smallest structural step: one critical section per `allow` call (read-modify-write under `self._lock`), delete `_snapshot`. This removes the whole class of check-then-act races rather than one instance. | Thread test + inspection. | high
A-2 | blocker | ratelimit/limiter.py:49-50, 84-85 | The trust boundary is inverted: a fault inside the limiter is converted into an admit. Any future bug (for example a bad `clock` or a corrupted bucket tuple) silently disables limiting. | Requirement 3 fails and bugs are masked. | `return False` in both. Do not also swallow `BaseException`; `Exception` is already the right breadth. | Fail-closed tests. | high
A-3 | high | ratelimit/limiter.py:40 together with :46 | Two representation choices interact badly: integer truncation of refill (`int(...)`) and resetting `last` on every call. Either alone is tolerable; together they make the refill rate depend on polling frequency, which is a correctness bug in the data model, not an edge case. | Sub-1/s rates (requirement 2) and also any rate when polled faster than 1/rate. | Store float tokens and advance `last` only on admit; the bucket then has a single source of truth for "credit since last admit". | Sub-1/s test. | high
A-4 | high | ratelimit/config.py:30-40 | Parsing and validation are conflated: types are checked only indirectly through `float()`/`int()` exceptions, positivity only for `rate`, and `KNOWN_KEYS` (:21) is declared but never consulted, so it is a dead source of truth. | Requirement 4 fails; the constant misleads maintainers into thinking unknown keys are rejected. | Consult `KNOWN_KEYS` first, then explicit type guards, then coercion, then range checks, each raising `ConfigError`. Keep it in `load_config`; no new module. | Config tests. | high
A-5 | low | ratelimit/limiter.py:60-62 vs :77 | Docstring says a stamp exactly `window` seconds old "has left the window and no longer counts"; code keeps `t >= cutoff`, so it still counts. Two sources of truth for the boundary. | Off-by-one at the window boundary for callers who read the docstring. | Either change `>=` to `>` or correct the docstring. This is a caller-observable behaviour change outside the four requirements, so flag for a product decision. | `SlidingWindow(1, 10.0)`: allow@0, advance 10.0, allow -> currently False. | high
